Entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-20261120**: sum up the
world's news of Friday 20 November 2026 in one image.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. No network, no clock, no randomness — two runs
write byte-identical output.

## The image

A 1024×1024 newspaper front page, dated **Friday 20 November
2026**, with the six stories that defined the day:

1. **Trump puts Iran decision off to 2027** — "I'm not rushing,"
   he says; new strikes on hold until after Jan 1.
2. **Three powers sign 'peace declaration'** — Nuclear war "cannot
   be won"; Ukraine and Iran go unmentioned.
3. **Cyclone Akvan heads for India's east coast** — Landfall due
   today near Gopalpur; tens of thousands evacuated.
4. **GTA 6 passes $1.84bn, topping The Odyssey** — Sensor Tower
   puts launch sales past the film's $1.80bn box office.
5. **Russian strikes hit two EU embassies in Kyiv** — Austrian and
   Dutch missions hit; one person killed.
6. **Voyager 1 a light-day from Earth** — First craft a light-day
   out; its signal takes a day to reach home.

Every headline was checked against reports published in the 20
November 2026 news cycle (see `SOURCES.md` in the build notes);
figures that conflicted across outlets were left out rather than
guessed.
